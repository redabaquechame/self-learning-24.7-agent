# SC-4 offline temporal media tutoring review

**Scope:** timestamped multimodal claims, processed coverage and gaps, timeline
invalidation, decisive evidence planning, current-app semantic transfer,
qualified-pack/practice lineage and explicit playback authority.  
**Review dimensions:** security, correctness, performance, maintainability.  
**Current verdict:** locally and natively verified for deterministic synthetic
contracts; provider, content-review and real native journey qualification remain
open.

## Resolved findings

1. **Sparse evidence could read as full-video understanding.** Every response
   carries processed intervals/timestamps, named limitations and
   `complete_source_understanding_claimed: false`. Screenshot-only whole-video
   requests remain partial.
2. **Narration could become visual fact.** Claim kind and modality are closed.
   Caption claims are reported/contested, visual claims need actual current frame
   IDs, and transitions need distinct before/after frames.
3. **Wrong subtitles could silently become truth.** A contested caption can
   support only a claim explicitly labelled contested.
4. **A seek could retain old advice.** Explanation identity includes timeline
   epoch and transition fingerprint. Missing or changed identity suppresses it;
   the selective mutation dropping the epoch check is caught.
5. **Serialized evidence could forge current lineage.** The consumer validates
   strict snapshot fields and rejects current caption/frame rows whose source
   revision or content digest differs.
6. **Fast transitions could be guessed.** Missing before/after coverage emits a
   bounded request plan marked `retrieval_performed: false`.
7. **Tutorial coordinates could be replayed in another app.** Transfer accepts
   no tutorial coordinates and calls current semantic grounding with current
   source, capture and geometry identity. Ambiguous controls abstain.
8. **A plausible candidate lesson could authorize practice.** Transfer requires
   qualified human-reviewed pack scope. The shipped pack passes mechanical
   validation but remains `CANDIDATE` and unassignable.
9. **Media speech could control playback.** Playback changes require an explicit,
   current, expiring user grant and return an unexecuted intent only.
10. **Content replacement could freeze expiry checks.** SC-4 integration removed
    the media-index clock replacement and added a live expiry regression.
