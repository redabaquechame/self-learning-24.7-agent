No blocker prevents SC-0A source/local work.

- Provider qualification: SC-1B's local and loopback implementation is verified,
  but T021 has not run because no explicit provider/model and spend budget are
  authorized. The packet remains unaccepted at
  `LOCAL_VERIFIED_REVIEWED_PROVIDER_BLOCKED` while independent packets continue.
- Native qualification: the exact isolated SDK and package graph are locally proven. The companion UI, vision/browser control, application fixtures, signed package, and device/display qualification remain future gates.
- Human evaluation: participants, reviewers, consent protocol, and study operations remain future prerequisites.
- Content review: SC-1C's reporting fixture is locally valid but no real independent subject-matter reviewer has approved its exact digest, so it is not assignable.
- Accessible assessment: SC-2B T040 requires a real native assessment with human assistive-technology users; offline policy tests cannot qualify that claim.
- Grounding qualification: SC-2A now has local and partial native synthetic transform, primitive-parser and stale-suppression evidence. It still needs a visible production overlay, real multi-monitor and mixed-DPI transitions, application-family and small-target measurements, capture-exclusion proof, and a separately qualified visual-grounding adapter.
- Safe-practice qualification: SC-2C may implement local mechanisms, but packet acceptance still depends on SC-2A and SC-2B qualification, a visible native teaching canvas, native no-change evidence against a real supported application fixture, and independent review of any subjective creative criteria.
- Safe-practice integration: SC-2C's local and native synthetic mechanisms pass, but practice lineage and answer keys are not yet derived from an authority-checked qualified assignment, and the native canvas is not connected to the authenticated visible companion request path.
- Audio qualification: SC-3 depends on SC-1B and SC-2B acceptance. No live microphone, realtime provider/model route, audio spend budget or device qualification is authorized; dependency-independent offline source separation, interruption, queue, fallback and Stop mechanics may continue.
- Audio integration: SC-3's local and native offline control mechanics pass, but no visible authenticated companion adapter connects them to a microphone, speaker or the existing metered provider gateway. Physical device/acoustic, provider latency/cost, content, human and accessibility qualification remain open.
- Flagship journey qualification: SC-2D depends on unaccepted SC-2A/SC-2B qualification and ultimately requires an installed visible onboarding run, novice participants, screen-reader/keyboard evidence and multi-monitor interruption testing. Dependency-independent session/view-model work may continue without upgrading those claims.
- Multilingual/accessibility qualification: SC-3A depends on unaccepted SC-3/SC-2D qualification. Locale contracts and fallback mechanics can proceed offline, but every language/accent/device needs separate provider accuracy, caption, comprehension, content-review and assistive-technology evidence before it is called qualified.

Authorized-source, timeline, caption-gap and bounded-sampling mechanics can proceed against synthetic bytes, but no external URL/upload/current-screen source or audiovisual provider result may be called qualified without exact rights, retrieved-byte, provider-budget and correlation evidence.
- Media-tutoring qualification: SC-4 depends on unaccepted SC-2A/SC-2B/SC-3/SC-4A qualification. Timestamp, modality and semantic app-transfer mechanics may proceed against synthetic evidence, but no complete-video understanding, real-application transfer, playback control, learning or production claim may be made without correlated native/provider/content-review evidence.
- Scoped-context qualification: SC-5A's platform-owned local/native synthetic mechanics pass and current primary-source research is recorded. SC-1C independent content review, real connector revocation/deletion and a visible installed private-source picker/account-switch/accessibility journey remain open. The optional Highlight adapter remains absent because the inspected public sources did not establish the required maintained commercial private-context integration contract.
- Open-world investigation qualification: SC-1D depends on the unaccepted SC-1B provider and SC-1C content-review tiers. Frozen offline unknown-application, citation, version and source-authority mechanics may continue; real provider quality and reviewed current documentation cannot be claimed from fixtures.
- Release: signing identity, deployment target, and production authorization remain future prerequisites.

These are open gates, not completed work. Continue independent local packets until a dependency actually reaches one of them.
